# CertaMaris Search-Intent and Cannibalization Review

Generated from the 2026-10-22 static SEO audit.

## Decision framework

Routes were treated as distinct only when they serve a different stage of buyer
research, a different audience, or a different operational task. Page-family
overlap alone was not treated as duplication.

## Consolidation decisions

- `/industries` permanently redirects to `/who-we-serve`.
- `/sample-platform` permanently redirects to `/demo`.
- `/privacy` permanently redirects to `/legal/privacy`.
- `/terms` permanently redirects to `/legal/terms`.
- `/trust/subprocessors` permanently redirects to `/legal/subprocessors`.
- `/platform/corporate-control-plane` permanently redirects to
  `/platform/client-company-portal`.

## Deferred consolidation

No product, solution, compliance, topic, or resource route was removed or
canonicalized into another page. Their titles, H1s, descriptions, and body
content express different research stages and operational tasks. The strict
audit found no duplicate titles or descriptions among indexable routes.

## Residual risk

Search Console query data was not available. If future query reports show two
retained pages competing for the same query, that should be resolved from
measured impressions and intent evidence, not from title similarity alone.
